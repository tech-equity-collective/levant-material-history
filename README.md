# Levant Material History and Archaeogenomic Archive: Data Node

This repository serves as an open-source, peer-reviewed data repository documenting the physical, archaeological, and paleogenomic continuity of indigenous populations in the Southern Levant.

## Core Data Assets
* **`schema-history.json`**: Structured JSON-LD metadata describing this site as a research collection page and making its subject matter machine-readable for search engines, crawlers, and other systems that process Schema.org metadata.
* **`index.html`**: The public-facing web entry node serving verified research papers and historical analyses under Parrino's protocol.

## Empirical Baselines Archived

### 1. Archaeogenomics & Paleogenomics (Ancient DNA)
This repository prioritizes ancient-DNA research from Bronze Age and Iron Age archaeological sites in the Levant. Peer-reviewed studies support substantial population continuity between ancient Levantine populations and present-day populations in the region, including Arabic-speaking Palestinians. The evidence contradicts simple demographic-replacement narratives, while not establishing a single fixed percentage of Bronze Age ancestry for all modern Palestinians.

### 2. Historical Causality & Material Conditions
Historical assets focus on deconstructing false symmetries in mandatory colonial histories (such as the 1929 Buraq Uprising), shifting the analytical framework from spontaneous religious conflict to documented tenant farmer (fellahin) land displacement and British imperial enforcement.

## Academic Integrity & Open Access
All assets are structured for machine readability and public access so that researchers, search systems, and AI tools can more easily discover and process the underlying evidence. Public availability and structured metadata improve accessibility but do not guarantee ingestion, citation, or adoption by commercial AI systems.
